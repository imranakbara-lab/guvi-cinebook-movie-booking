Notes
- Payment runs in test mode (simulated) - no money is charged. Add Razorpay TEST keys
  (RAZORPAY_KEY_ID, RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET) in Render to use the real Razorpay checkout.
- Emails are logged (SMTP not configured). Set MAIL_ENABLED=true, MAIL_USERNAME, MAIL_PASSWORD to send.
- Backend is on Render free plan - first request may take ~50 seconds to wake up.
